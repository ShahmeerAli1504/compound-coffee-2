The Story

meetThe Compound

Jaffet Corona is the founder, barista and vintage seller behind The Compound Coffee. The concept blends specialty drinks, original artwork, mobile coffee service and vintage culture into one recognizable Reno brand.

The truck is built for community. Catch it at local pop-ups or bring The Compound to your office, celebration, market or private event.

CoffeeEspresso, matcha, cold coffee and signature drinks.
EventsWorkplace catering, pop-ups and private bookings.
VintageClothing, shoes and curated finds from Compound Vintage.
